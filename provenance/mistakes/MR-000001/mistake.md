# MR-000001: Starter pack missed risk acceptance links

## Summary
The starter pack shipped without risk acceptance and mistake examples, so enforcement
guidance could not demonstrate cross-linking.

## Trigger
CI highlighted missing risk acceptance links in sample governance records.

## Root Cause
Governance templates were updated without adding the supporting risk and mistake records.

## Prevent Rule
Decision records must reference existing Mistake Records and guardrail IDs that are declared
by those Mistake Records.
